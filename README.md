# 2. Why I changed it this way

Your current opening is:

> Full-Stack Developer & AI Student @ Johannes Kepler University Linz, Austria  
> Building Construvisio...

That tells people:

**"I'm a student building a construction SaaS."**

But that's no longer the strongest story.

The new opening says:

> **Full-Stack Developer · AI Automation Engineer · Founder @ CruziLux**

Then immediately:

> I build full-stack applications, AI-powered systems, and business automation tools.

That tells a recruiter, client, founder, or potential collaborator **exactly what kind of developer you are.**

---

# 3. Emma becomes your flagship

This is the biggest change.

Instead of:

```text
🔨 What I'm Building

Construvisio

we now have:

🚀 What I'm Building

🤖 Emma — AI Receptionist

That's much more aligned with where you're going.

And importantly, we're not deleting Construvisio.

It's moved down into:

📌 Selected Projects

So your previous work still demonstrates your engineering ability.

4. CruziLux gets its own section

This is important for your personal identity.

We don't want:

Jesal = CruziLux

Instead:

Jesal → Founder/Builder → CruziLux → Products

That gives you room to build other things in the future.

And when someone sees your GitHub, they can understand that CruziLux isn't just a random repository. It's the business/product ecosystem you're building.

5. I intentionally removed some things

For example, I wouldn't put:

Open to Werkstudent positions (15–20h/week)

on your GitHub README anymore.

That's useful information for your CV and LinkedIn, but your GitHub should primarily communicate:

What can you build?

rather than:

What job are you looking for?

Someone looking at your GitHub should see your capability first.

You can still keep:

📍 Linz, Austria

because that's useful context.

6. Your Tech Stack should also change

Your old version has:

React, Next.js, Flutter, Tailwind CSS
Node.js, Express, Django, Supabase
PostgreSQL, MongoDB, MySQL, SQLite
Claude API, Gemini API, n8n

That's fine, but it looks like a list of technologies.

Backend

AI & Automation

Much easier to scan.

And I added FastAPI and WebSockets, because those are particularly relevant to the systems you've been building.
